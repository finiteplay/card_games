# FreeCell by FinitePlay

## Main store listing (en-US)

**Title:** FreeCell Solitaire: FinitePlay

**Short description:** Simple, battery-conscious offline FreeCell: solvable deals, hints, no ads

**Full description:**

See every card and plan your next move in classic FreeCell. Use four free cells and empty columns
to make space, move card sequences, and explore different approaches with undo and replay.

The FinitePlay promise: completely free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.
Play stays completely on your device: no accounts, tracking, data collection or sharing,
leaderboards, or online features. Your progress and statistics stay private and local.

Every included deal starts with a verified solution. A later board can still reach a dead end,
and hints have search limits; when a hint cannot establish a path, it says so plainly.

Free cells and empty columns let you move card sequences when there is enough space; the game
works out the legal capacity for you.

Play at your own pace. Configurable rest reminders invite you to pause after extended play.
There are no urgency bonuses or timed daily rewards. Local scores and statistics help you
reflect on your play, without competitive leaderboards or pressure to keep going.

Features:
• 2,000 certified-solvable hands
• Optional hints that search your current board
• Tap or drag controls, including multi-card moves
• Undo, Replay, and automatic finish
• Optional automatic foundation moves, sound, and card animations
• Light, dark, and time-based themes
• On-device statistics and personal-best comparisons
• 30 interface languages

FinitePlay makes quality games that respect your time, privacy, and attention.
