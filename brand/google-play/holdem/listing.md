# Texas Hold'em by FinitePlay

## Main store listing (en-US)

**Title:** Texas Holdem Poker: FinitePlay

**Short description:** Simple, battery-conscious offline poker: virtual chips, 5 opponents, no ads

**Full description:**

Play a six-seat Texas Hold'em poker tournament offline against five computer opponents,
with virtual chips and hints that explain the choices. Never real money: chips have no
monetary value, and there are no real-money bets, deposits, chip purchases, cash-outs, or prizes.

The FinitePlay promise: completely free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.
Play stays completely on your device: no accounts, tracking, data collection or sharing,
leaderboards, online features, or matches against real people. Your tournament progress and
statistics stay private and local.

Optional hints explain the idea behind a suggested action, helping beginners understand
hand strength, position, estimated equity, and pot odds. The advice uses visible information;
it cannot see opponents' hidden cards and does not guarantee a winning hand.

Every seat starts with the same stack of virtual chips. Play through rising blinds,
make your betting decisions, and aim to be the last player at the table.
Save your tournament and return to it later.

Play at your own pace. Configurable rest reminders invite you to pause after extended play.
There are no urgency bonuses or timed daily rewards, and no competitive leaderboards or
pressure to keep going. Designed for adults aged 18 and over.

Features:
• Five computer opponents with distinct playing styles
• Fold, check, call, bet, raise, and go all-in
• Bet-sizing controls and side-pot settlement
• Hints with estimated equity and pot odds
• Saved tournaments and on-device statistics
• Rules, hand rankings, and strategy help
• Portrait and landscape layouts
• Theme, sound, animation, and language settings
• 30 interface languages

FinitePlay makes card games that respect your time, privacy, and attention.
