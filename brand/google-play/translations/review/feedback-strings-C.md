## Needs a native speaker

- **All locales:** check that `feedback_include_info` fits on one or two lines next to the checkbox at large font scale; Indonesian, Vietnamese and Hindi are the longest.
- **ar / he:** confirm `%1$s` (a Latin email address) displays correctly inside RTL sentences, especially the trailing period after the address in `feedback_no_email_app`. Arabic uses masculine singular address (as the existing strings do); Hebrew uses plural address (as `level_up_title` does). Hebrew uses דוא״ל for email; some players may prefer מייל.
- **hi:** `enjoy_no` = ज़्यादा नहीं ("not much") as the soft no; `feedback_category_idea` = सुझाव (suggestion) rather than विचार; "खेल" vs "गेम" follows the existing strings.
- **th:** `enjoy_yes`/`enjoy_no` answer the question with ชอบ / ไม่ค่อยชอบ ("like" / "don't really like") instead of a literal yes/no; confirm this reads naturally as buttons.
- **ja:** `enjoy_no` = いまひとつ; confirm it is soft enough and fits the button.
- **ko:** `enjoy_no` = 그저 그래요 ("so-so") chosen over the blunter 별로요; `feedback_message_label` is just 메시지.
- **zh-CN / zh-TW:** Yes/No buttons answer the question (喜欢 / 不太喜欢, 喜歡 / 不太喜歡). zh-TW uses 意見回饋, 傳送, 電子郵件應用程式 and 連上網路 (Taiwan usage); confirm 意見回饋 vs 回饋.
- **id / vi:** `enjoy_no` = Tidak juga / Không hẳn; Vietnamese category `Khác` is very short ("Other") by design.
