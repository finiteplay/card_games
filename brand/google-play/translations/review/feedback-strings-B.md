## Needs a native speaker

- **All locales:** whether the soft refusal for `enjoy_no` reads as polite and natural (da "Ikke rigtig", fi "Ei oikeastaan", tr "Pek değil", el "Όχι ιδιαίτερα", cs "Ani ne", ro "Nu prea", hu "Nem igazán", bg "Не съвсем", hr "Baš i ne", sk "Ani nie").
- **Fit:** `feedback_include_info` is long in el ("Προσθήκη έκδοσης εφαρμογής, γλώσσας και μοντέλου συσκευής", 57 chars), bg (59 chars) and ro (61 chars) versus 46 in English; check the checkbox wraps cleanly at the smallest width. hr `about_send_feedback` "Pošalji povratne informacije" (28) is also long for a settings row.
- **tr:** register is mixed in the existing file (sen vs. siz); these strings use "sen". Pick one for the locale.
- **ro:** "feedback" loanword versus "părere" / "impresii".
- **bg:** "отзив" for feedback (alternative "обратна връзка"); "Добави ..." imperative versus a noun form for the checkbox.
- **el:** "εφαρμογή email" versus "εφαρμογή ηλεκτρονικού ταχυδρομείου"; "σχόλια" for feedback.
- **hu:** "Valami más" versus "Egyéb" for `feedback_category_other`; "ide: %1$s" phrasing in `feedback_no_email_app`.
- **cs / sk:** gender of "hra" is feminine, so `enjoy_body` uses "ji/ju najít/nájsť"; confirm it reads as referring to the game.
- **da:** "Kan du lide spillet?" for "Enjoying this game?" (alternative "Er du glad for spillet?").
