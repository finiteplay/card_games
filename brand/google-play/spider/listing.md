# Spider by FinitePlay

## Main store listing (en-US)

**Title:** Spider Solitaire: FinitePlay

**Short description:** Simple, battery-conscious offline Spider: 1/2/4 suits, hints, no ads

**Full description:**

Build complete King-to-Ace runs in Spider Solitaire. Start with 1 suit or take on 2 or 4 suits,
with undo, replay, and optional hints when you want help.

The FinitePlay promise: completely free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.
Play stays completely on your device: no accounts, tracking, data collection or sharing,
leaderboards, or online features. Your progress and statistics stay private and local.

Every included starting deal has a verified solution. Your choices still matter: a later position
may no longer be winnable. Guided hints can follow a known solution; after you choose another
path, finding a new solution is subject to search limits.

Play at your own pace. Configurable rest reminders invite you to pause after extended play.
There are no urgency bonuses or timed daily rewards. Local statistics help you reflect on
your play, without competitive leaderboards or pressure to keep going.

Features:
• One-, two-, and four-suit Spider
• 23,675 certified-solvable deals across all three suit counts
• Tap or drag complete same-suit sequences
• Move highlighting and optional guided hints
• Undo, Replay, and animated row deals and card moves
• Optional sound and animation controls
• Light, dark, and time-based themes
• On-device statistics
• 30 interface languages

FinitePlay makes quality games that respect your time, privacy, and attention.
