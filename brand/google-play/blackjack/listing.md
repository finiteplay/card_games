# Blackjack by FinitePlay

## Main store listing (en-US)

**Title:** Blackjack 21: FinitePlay

**Short description:** Play blackjack offline with virtual chips and optional basic-strategy hints

**Full description:**

Play blackjack offline against a computer dealer, with virtual chips and helpful explanations
for beginners. Never real money: chips have no monetary value, and there are no real-money
bets, deposits, chip purchases, cash-outs, or prizes.

The FinitePlay promise: completely free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.
Play stays completely on your device: no accounts, tracking, data collection or sharing,
leaderboards, online features, or matches against real people. Your progress and statistics
stay private and local.

Play classic blackjack with clear rules, fair dealing, and optional basic-strategy guidance.
Blackjack by FinitePlay includes hit, stand, double, split, insurance, dealer peek, and 3:2
blackjack payouts.

Practice decisions with optional basic-strategy hints that explain why to hit, stand, double,
or split. Learn from the dealer's up card, your hand, and bust-risk explanations, then track
your virtual bankroll and statistics. Hints help you understand choices; they do not guarantee wins.

Play at your own pace. Configurable rest reminders invite you to pause after extended play.
There are no urgency bonuses or timed daily rewards, and no competitive leaderboards or
pressure to keep going. Designed for adults aged 18 and over.

FinitePlay makes card games that respect your time, privacy, and attention.
