## Third-party sound assets

The bundled sound effects listed in `docs/SOUND_ASSETS.md` are dedicated to the public domain
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. They include card and chip recordings by Kenney Vleugels
(Kenney.nl), a card-riffle recording by Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero), and generated/procedural cues
distributed by SFXMint. The shared win jingle is by Fupi. Attribution is not required; FinitePlay
records it here and in the asset manifest for provenance.
